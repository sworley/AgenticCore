## Note

The decision previously recorded here - making the `allowed_in_adopter_repos`
metadata flag the authoritative gate for what ships into an adopter repo - was
folded into the governing adoption/distribution model as **ADR-0015 D-9**
(amendment, sourced from AC-0014 Stage 1 + Stage 2). ADR-0015 is the governing
decision for the shipped adoption surface (D-1/D-7/D-8), so the change belongs
there rather than as a separate decision. See `mddocs/adr/adr-0015-framework-distribution-adoption-model.md`.
